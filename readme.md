# 🌐 GlobaLens AI

**Real-time, multi-agent AI platform for global supply chain risk monitoring and predictive insights.**  
Built for **HackTheAI 2025** using **SmythOS Flow Builder**, **FastAPI**, and **Next.js**.

---

## 🚀 Elevator Pitch
GlobaLens AI provides **instant, actionable risk intelligence** for global supply chains.  
Our SmythOS-powered agents monitor news, social signals and logistics data in real time,  
predict upcoming disruptions, and recommend mitigation strategies—helping decision makers reduce losses and act fast.

---

## ✨ Key Features
- **Web Scraping Risk Monitor Agent** – Collects live data from supply-chain sources and outputs structured JSON.
- **Supply Chain Disruption Aggregator Agent** – Filters & analyzes disruption events and creates real-time alerts and executive summaries.
- **Predictive Insights & Visualization Agent** – Generates predictive metrics and visualization-ready JSON for dashboards and reports.
- **Dynamic Risk Heatmap & Timeline** – Interactive global map and trend charts.
- **PDF/CSV Export & Dark/Light Mode** – One-click export and accessible UI.
